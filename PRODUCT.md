## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

IAVSE brings community exchange and resource discovery together, with personal blog spaces for publishing work and writing. These priorities were explicitly confirmed by the user in this conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing website offers community sharing, discussion, bookmarks and following; resource directories include UI designs, Skills, documentation, websites and tools.
- Blog spaces use the site's built-in templates, visual editor and publishing workflow.
- Keep source and license information for third-party resources. Existing material must not be presented as universally reusable or officially endorsed.
- Preserve user data, publishing permissions, account flows, real destinations and authored blog appearances.
- The latest user instruction is to base the entire frontend on the public [RBP Portfolio repository](https://github.com/DavidHDev/rbp-portfolio) and [demo](https://rbp-portfolio.vercel.app/). This supersedes the earlier homepage-only Cloudlight/Security blend, Gradient Bars experiment, optical tide scene and protected-opening constraints. The adaptation covers the shared application system while retaining task-oriented layouts for editing and administration.

## Brand Commitments

Retain the IAVSE name, existing outlined V identity, blue focus accent and English mission “Insight through AI. Vigilance for Opportunity. Seize to Evolve.” The user's binding visual direction is: “动效也要保持不变呀，还有排版，都跟这个开源仓库保持一致，就是内容合并过来.” For the template-backed public pages, preserve the pinned RBP Portfolio composition, spacing, responsive behavior and normal-motion parameters, replacing the sample content with actual IAVSE content. The published reference's computed system-ui font stack, including its serif-class fallback, is the typography authority.

The public shell has the original centered, fixed three-link capsule and theme control; account and language access are in Contact. The homepage is Hero → four Projects → Contact; About and the resource/blog overviews use the upstream About and Projects page structures. Do not reintroduce a full-width branded header or the earlier custom homepage workbench. Existing identity assets remain available without inserting new branding into the template composition. Native community, catalog, account, administration and editor layouts keep their working behavior; authored blog appearances remain independent. Those operational views have no upstream page equivalent, so this commitment does not claim pixel identity for them. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and the [Portfolio implementation notes](docs/portfolio-frontend.md).

## Evidence on Hand

Actual resource records live in the existing catalogs; public community content comes from the qualified public feed. Blog template examples are fictional sample content rendered by the site's real template factory. Do not invent customers, testimonials, user totals or engagement figures.
