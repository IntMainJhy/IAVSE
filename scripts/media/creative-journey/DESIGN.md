# Three product stories

Keep the site's calm product typography and optical identity, while giving each destination a distinct film.

- Home: cool silver, a broad visual-reference collage, an open resource directory, then the creator's own published page. Show the platform's path from idea to output.
- Blogs: pale sage and warm paper, a real canvas interaction, a three-column gallery of bundled examples, then a paired desktop/mobile preview. Show personal expression rather than resource discovery.
- Resources: deep slate opening, a visible design index, readable close-ups of two real Skill recommendations, and the tools directory. Show information users can actually find.

Headline type is 59–78 px with −.035em tracking at 1280×720. Headlines have independent zones; film controls and the localized explanation remain outside the video. Keep movement directed: one scene or subject settles at a time, with a short transition at 4.1 s and 8.6 s. Avoid endlessly orbiting fake UI or introducing unsupported product controls.

The title text and screenshots do not change language inside the video; accessible chapter text, descriptions, controls and links outside it remain localized in Chinese, English and Japanese.
