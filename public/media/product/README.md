# Product cinema assets

Three distinct silent product films authored locally with HyperFrames 0.9.20 on 2026-11-08. Reproducible source and commands: `scripts/media/creative-journey/README.md`.

- `iavse-creative-journey.mp4`: Home — design references, useful knowledge, the creator's published space.
- `iavse-blog-journey.mp4`: Blog Spaces — actual canvas color editing, bundled example styles, desktop/mobile preview.
- `iavse-resource-journey.mp4`: Resources — actual UI design index, specific Skill recommendations, websites and tools.
- Each `.webp` is its own film's poster extracted at 2.8 seconds.

1280×720, 30 fps, 14 seconds, H.264, no audio. The films use original IAVSE design examples, authentic public product screenshots, and the site's existing creator/example homepage captures. No third-party stock video or invented product UI. Sources retain their existing attribution. DM Sans is licensed under OFL; source font and license are included in the authoring project. The small English film labels supplement the localized HTML descriptions and links.
